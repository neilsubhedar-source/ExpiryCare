    Expiry dates are easy to miss

    Most households and small institutions rely on memory or scattered notes. That creates real, everyday problems.

    Manual tracking is easy to forget

    Sticky notes and mental reminders don't scale once the number of products grows.

    Products get forgotten

    Items pushed to the back of a shelf are often rediscovered only after they've expired.

    Health risk from expired items

    Using expired medicines or personal-care products unknowingly can pose avoidable health risks.

    Unnecessary waste and cost

    Products discarded after expiry represent money and resources that didn't need to be lost.
