Product story

How ExpiryCare works

ExpiryCare gives you one place to record, monitor and act on expiry-sensitive products, so you always know what's safe to use and what needs attention.

Get Started Login
  • Active

    More than 30 days left before expiry.

  • Expiring Soon

    30 days or fewer left. Needs planning.

  • Expired

    Past the expiry date. Do not use.

The core workflow

Add → Track → Get alerts → Take action. Hover or tab through the steps.

  1. Add a product

    Enter the product name, category, quantity, purchase date and expiry date.

  2. Automatic status tracking

    Each product is grouped as Active, Expiring Soon or Expired based on its expiry date.

  3. Expiry alerts

    Products approaching their expiry date appear in your Alerts page automatically.

  4. Take action

    Use, replace or safely discard the product, then update or remove the entry.

What you can do with ExpiryCare

Maintain an inventory

A searchable, filterable record of every expiry-sensitive product you own.

Monitor expiry status

See at a glance which items are active, nearing expiry, or already expired.

Get actionable alerts

Alerts link directly to the product, so you can act without hunting.

Read safety guidance

General guidance on medicines, food and personal-care products.

Designed to be simple and honest

ExpiryCare is a student prototype. Everything runs in your browser using local storage, and the insights are simple rule-based summaries of your own data — not artificial intelligence.

  • Plain, readable status rules
  • No fake alerts or invented statistics
  • Ready to connect to a Flask + Firebase backend later
  • Keyboard and screen-reader friendly

Please note: Healthcare and product-safety guidance provided within ExpiryCare is for general informational awareness only and does not replace professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
